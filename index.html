07FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is this insurance advice or a coverage guarantee?

No. SubReady compares documents against your contract's stated requirements and records the result with dates. It is not insurance advice, it does not place or interpret coverage for you, and it does not guarantee that a policy will respond to a claim. The decision to start, hold, or decline a subcontractor is always yours.

The sub sent a certificate of insurance. Is that not enough?

A certificate is evidence supplied by a third party; it is not the policy. Additional insured coverage, for example, is created by the endorsement — insurers themselves explain that the certificate alone does not confer it. The packet compares what the documents show with what your contract requires, and marks the difference item by item.

How do you check WSIB status?

From the clearance certificate you provide, and — where a permitted official route exists and you authorize it — by a confirmation recorded with its date and source. We never scrape WSIB or registry sites, bypass logins, or work around CAPTCHAs. If a confirmation cannot be completed, the item is marked UNVERIFIED. It is never upgraded on the strength of the document alone.

How long does it take?

The standard turnaround is 2 business days from a complete submission. If a confirmation depends on a broker or insurer responding, we tell you which item is waiting rather than holding the whole packet without explanation.

What do I need to provide?

Your contract's insurance requirements for the project — the intake walks through them — and whatever the sub has sent you so far: certificate of insurance, endorsements, WSIB clearance. Documents you do not have yet are recorded as missing, and the packet includes the wording to request them.
